How Do Stars Form?

A guided flight through the places we study — Ginsburg group, University of Florida

Massive stars build the elements, light up galaxies, and end as supernovae. We still do not know what decides how many of them a cloud of gas will make. This tour flies you through the telescope images we use to find out — no astronomy background needed.
